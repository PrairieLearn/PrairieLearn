5 points. A fountain fills a 1.8-liter bottle in 12 seconds at a constant rate. Find the flow rate in liters per minute. Include the unit in your answer.

$$q=\frac{1.8\ \mathrm{L}}{12\ \mathrm{s}}\times\frac{60\ \mathrm{s}}{1\ \mathrm{min}}=9\ \mathrm{L/min}.$$

Equivalent flow-rate units are acceptable if the conversion is correct.
