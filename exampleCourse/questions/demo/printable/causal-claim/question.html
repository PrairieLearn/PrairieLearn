5 points. After a campus installs bottle-filling stations, bottled-water sales fall by 18%. During the same month, the campus store increases bottled-water prices.

A report concludes: “The new stations caused the entire 18% decrease.” In three or four sentences, explain why that conclusion is too strong and propose one useful comparison or additional measurement.

Example response: Sales may have fallen partly because the higher price reduced demand. This before-and-after observation does not separate the effects of price and station availability. Compare the change with similar stores whose prices changed but whose station access did not, or use a design that varies station access while holding prices constant.

Grading (5 points): identify the price change as an alternative explanation (2); explain the limitation of the causal claim (1); propose a relevant comparison or measurement (2). Other justified designs are acceptable.
