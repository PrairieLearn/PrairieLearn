6 points. A tank initially holds $V_0$ liters. Water enters at a constant rate $r$ liters per minute and leaves at a constant rate $u$ liters per minute. Assume the tank neither empties nor overflows during the interval.

Write an expression for the volume $V(t)$, in liters, after $t$ minutes. Use $V_0$, $r$, $u$, and $t$.

$$V(t)=V_0+(r-u)t.$$

The net inflow is $r-u$. Multiplying by time gives the change in volume, which is added to the initial volume.
