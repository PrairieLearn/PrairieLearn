4 points. A team measures a fountain's flow rate by timing how long it takes to fill a marked container. Select all changes that would improve the reliability or accuracy of the measurements.

 Repeat the measurement several times and report the mean. Round every time measurement to the nearest ten seconds. Check the container's volume markings against a calibrated measuring vessel. Use the same clear start and stop rule for every trial. Discard every reading below the result the team expected.

Repeating trials reduces the influence of random variation; calibration addresses systematic error; and a consistent timing rule reduces operator variation. Coarse rounding loses information, and selective removal of unexpected readings introduces bias.
