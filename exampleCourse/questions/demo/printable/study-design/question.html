4 points. The facilities team wants to compare water use with a standard faucet and a low-flow faucet. Which study design best isolates the effect of faucet type?

Select one answer.

 Compare a busy dining hall with standard faucets to a quiet library with low-flow faucets. Randomly assign comparable washrooms to the two faucet types, then measure use over the same weeks. Measure standard faucets during term and low-flow faucets during a holiday. Ask volunteers whether they think low-flow faucets save water.

Random assignment to comparable washrooms, measured over the same period, reduces systematic differences between groups. Building use, season, and self-reported opinions would otherwise confound the comparison.
