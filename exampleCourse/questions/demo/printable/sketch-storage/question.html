8 points. An initially empty tank receives water at $2\ \mathrm{L/min}$ for four minutes. The inlet then closes, and water drains at $1\ \mathrm{L/min}$ for the next four minutes.

Sketch the stored volume for $0\leq x\leq8$. The horizontal coordinate $x$ is time in minutes; the vertical coordinate $y$ is volume in liters. Show the change in slope and both endpoints.

Join $(0,0)$ to $(4,8)$ with a straight rising line, then join $(4,8)$ to $(8,4)$ with a straight falling line.

Grading (8 points): initial point (1); first straight segment with slope $+2$ (2); peak at $(4,8)$ (2); second straight segment with slope $-1$ (2); endpoint $(8,4)$ (1). Accept a clear hand-drawn equivalent.
