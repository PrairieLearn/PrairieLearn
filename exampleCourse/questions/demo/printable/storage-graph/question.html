6 points. The graph shows the volume of water in a tank during a 30-minute observation. Straight line segments connect the observations.

Find (a) the net inflow rate during the first 10 minutes, and (b) the net change in stored volume over all 30 minutes. Use a positive sign for a gain and a negative sign for a loss.

(a) $(600-200)/10=40\ \mathrm{L/min}$.

(b) $300-200=+100\ \mathrm{L}$. Net change compares final and initial volume, rather than adding the magnitudes of each rise and fall.
