6 points. Arrange these actions to measure a fountain's flow rate. The container is empty before the procedure starts. Use every action once.

 Place the empty marked container under the fountain outlet. Start the flow and timer together. Stop the flow and timer when the water reaches the target mark. Record the collected volume and elapsed time. Divide the recorded volume by the elapsed time to obtain flow rate.

The container must be in position before timing starts. Collect the measurement before recording it, then calculate the rate from the recorded values.
