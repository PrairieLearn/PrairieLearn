6 points. A roof has area $80\ \mathrm{m^2}$. A storm deposits $15\ \mathrm{mm}$ of rain, and the collection system captures 75% of the water that falls on the roof. Assume all captured water fits in storage.

Build a four-step calculation for the captured volume in liters. At each step with alternatives, choose the valid statement.
