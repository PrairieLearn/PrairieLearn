A rectangular garden has area $48\,\mathrm{m}^2$ and perimeter $28\,\mathrm{m}$. Determine its side lengths. Show the equations, solve them, and draw a labelled rectangle.

For the online version, photograph your working and attach a one-page PDF summary. For the printed version, write the same work in the spaces below.

Let the sides be $x$ and $y$. Then $xy=48$ and $x+y=14$. Thus $x(14-x)=48$, so $(x-6)(x-8)=0$. The sides are $6\,\mathrm{m}$ and $8\,\mathrm{m}$.

Check: $6\times8=48$ and $2(6+8)=28$.
