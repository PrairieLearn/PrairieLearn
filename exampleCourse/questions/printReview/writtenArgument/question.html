A school proposes replacing a small lawn with a rain garden. Write a short recommendation that:

  1. Explains how the change could reduce stormwater runoff.
  2. Identifies one maintenance cost or trade-off.
  3. Proposes a measurable outcome for evaluating the project.

Use approximately 100–150 words. Explain the reasoning behind your recommendation.

Example response. A rain garden can retain runoff temporarily and allow more water to infiltrate into soil. Plant roots and soil may also trap sediment. The school should budget for weeding, seasonal maintenance, and occasional replacement of plants. A pilot installation is justified if the site drains safely away from buildings. Compare runoff volume after similar rainfall events before and after installation, and record how long standing water remains. These measurements test performance more directly than appearance alone.

Rubric: mechanism (2 points), realistic trade-off (1 point), measurable evaluation with a baseline (2 points).
